About Us

Resources for GCSE Maths, GCSE Physics, A-Level Maths, Further Maths and A-Level Physics — built for students and teachers.

I’m Warren David Thomas. I’ve taught Maths and Physics up to A-Level. I have over 10 years of teaching experience, and I presently teach A-Level Maths and Further Maths.

Calcurion is for students and teachers — free exam-style questions, worked solutions, lessons and worksheets across GCSE and A-Level Maths, Further Maths, and Physics, so learners can practise with confidence and aim high.

If you’ve found the site useful, I’m glad. We’re human, so if you spot an error, please email calcurion@gmail.com and I’ll look into it and correct it.
